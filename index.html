What types of websites do you build?+

I build landing pages, WordPress websites, business websites, and custom websites based on the needs of each project.

Can you build a custom website?+

Yes. Depending on the project’s requirements, I can create a custom-coded website using suitable web technologies.

Are the websites responsive?+

Yes. Websites are designed to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

How long does it take to build a website?+

The timeline depends on the website’s size, features, content, and requirements. A simple landing page may take a few days, while larger websites can require more time.

Can I update my website later?+

WordPress websites can be built so you can update content through WordPress. Custom websites may require code changes, depending on how they are built.
